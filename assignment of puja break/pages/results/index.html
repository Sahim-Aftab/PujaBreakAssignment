How to read these examples

  • Check the exam and yearReal results should identify the examination, year, and official source.
  • Distinguish score from rankMarks, percentiles, and ranks are different measures and should not be treated as interchangeable.
  • Verify before sharingAny real result requires consent and confirmation against official documents.
  • Avoid guaranteesA sample scorecard cannot predict a student's future result or admission outcome.
