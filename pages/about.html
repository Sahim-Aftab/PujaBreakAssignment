About the Institution

Ajmal Super 40 was established under the aegis of Ajmal Foundation with a vision to nurture underprivileged rural and urban talent. We believe every capable student deserves world-class mentorship irrespective of financial constraints.

Our Educational Mission

  1. Identify Talent: Screen and identify promising students through fair entrance assessments.
  2. Provide Quality Mentorship: Deliver specialized preparation for engineering and medical entrance exams.
  3. Inculcate Values: Build strong moral character, discipline, and community responsibility.
  4. Ensure Equal Opportunity: Remove socio-economic barriers to higher education.

Vision for the Future

Our goal is to expand the reach of competitive examination coaching, making advanced scientific education accessible across the region.
