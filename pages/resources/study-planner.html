STUDY ROUTINE · FLEXIBLE TEMPLATE

A weekly plan you can actually repeat

Use this as a starting point, then adapt the blocks to your class schedule, strengths, and energy.

Sample plan: This is a general planning template, not a prescribed number of study hours or a guaranteed path to an exam result. Protect sleep, meals, breaks, and time away from study.

Suggested weekly rhythm

  • Monday · Learn & recallReview one new concept from each priority subject, then close your notes and write what you remember.
  • Tuesday · Guided practiceAttempt a short set of topic questions. Mark uncertain answers and bring specific doubts to class.
  • Wednesday · Strengthen foundationsRevisit prerequisite ideas and redo a few previously missed questions without checking the solution first.
  • Thursday · Mixed practiceCombine recent topics in a timed practice block and record accuracy as well as time spent.
  • Friday · Revision & questionsUse flashcards, diagrams, or a formula sheet to review; resolve the week's outstanding doubts.
  • Saturday · Mock & reviewComplete a suitable timed test, then review mistakes calmly and write down one adjustment for next week.
  • Sunday · Rest & resetTake meaningful downtime, prepare materials for the coming week, and keep any review session light.

Keep the feedback loop simple

  1. Choose a small number of clear weekly goals.
  2. Track what you attempted and what still feels unclear.
  3. Review errors by cause: concept gap, recall, calculation, or time management.
  4. Adjust the next week's plan instead of trying to make up every missed hour.
