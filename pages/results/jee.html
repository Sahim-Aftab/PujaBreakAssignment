Read JEE outcomes carefully

  • Percentile is not percentageA percentile describes relative performance in a session; it is not the share of questions answered correctly.
  • Keep exam stages distinctJEE Main and JEE Advanced use separate processes, criteria, and result documents.
  • Ranks need contextCategory, eligibility, seat availability, and current counselling rules affect admissions.
  • Refer to official sourcesAlways check the official exam authority and counselling notices for current information.
